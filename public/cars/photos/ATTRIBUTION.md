# Car photos attribution

Photos under `public/cars/photos/` are sourced primarily from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) under their respective
Creative Commons / free licenses.

They are used for educational illustration in CarWise and are **not** live
dealer or manufacturer marketing assets. Some models use the closest
available freely licensed exterior photo when an exact India-spec image
was unavailable.

Do **not** scrape Google Images for copyrighted manufacturer photos.
